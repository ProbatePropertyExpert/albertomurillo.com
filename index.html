Probate Specialist

Your Family Deserves a Guide, Not Just an Agent.

When a loved one passes and leaves a home behind, most families do not know where to start. The legal process, the court timelines, the creditor notices, the Texas-specific rules -- it can feel impossible on top of grief. That is exactly where I come in.

  • Texas-specific expertise: Independent Administration, Muniment of Title, Small Estate Affidavit
  • Bexar County Probate Court knowledge -- I know the process and the timeline
  • Medicaid Estate Recovery (MERP) guidance -- the hidden claim most families miss
  • Homestead exemption transfer -- protect your family from a surprise tax bill
  • 4-year filing deadline awareness -- do not let the clock run out
  • Full network: probate attorneys, CPAs, estate sale companies, contractors
  • Bilingual service -- English and Spanish
